## Lab Questions

### What is difference between edge and level sensitive circuits?
Edge sensitive circuits respond only when the clock changes from LOW to HIGH (rising edge) or HIGH to LOW (falling edge) and level sensitive circuits respond continously to the input as long as the clock is at a particular level (HIGH or LOW).

### Why is it important to declare initial state?
It tells the circuit what value to start with which helps prevent unknown outputs when the circuit first operates.

### What do edge sensitive circuits let us build?
It helps us build flip flops and sequential circuits that store and update data at specific clock edges.
